What it is

A free Stunt Car Racer remake for the web and Mac.

Stunt Car Racer for Lovers is an open remake of Geoff Crammond’s classic Amiga stunt racing game. Race elevated tracks, clear jumps, and challenge friends in two-player — with optional Amiga+ physics and an Enhanced Look that stays inside the original flat-shaded SCR colour world.

Play instantly at nmarchand73.github.io/StuntCarRacerForLovers/play, or download the Apple Silicon Mac DMG. Source code: GitHub. Built on StuntCarRemake.

Inside the build

Same dusty Amiga world. More life on the rails.

Enhanced Look stays inside Geoff Crammond’s palette — flat shades, no photoreal. Toggle it off anytime with O.

  • Amiga+Vesuri-tuned springs and damping for that airtime feel.
  • EnhancedHorizon rings, rails, floodlights, clouds, cube field.
  • Two-playerLocal races, plus web guest/host over WebRTC.
  • PacksClassic · TNT · Original · Loops from the track menu.

FAQ

Common questions

What is Stunt Car Racer for Lovers?

A free modern remake of Geoff Crammond’s Amiga classic Stunt Car Racer, with two-player races, Amiga+ physics, Speed Feel, and Enhanced Look scenery that stays in the original SCR palette.

Can I play Stunt Car Racer online for free?

Yes — open the browser build. No account or install required.

How do I install it on Mac?

Get the Apple Silicon DMG, drag the app to Applications, then right-click → Open once (unsigned build).

Is this the original Amiga game?

No — it is a remake based on StuntCarRemake, preserving the flat-shaded Crammond feel for modern machines.

What do U, I, and O do?

U Amiga+ physics · I Speed Feel · O Enhanced Look · P Pause.
